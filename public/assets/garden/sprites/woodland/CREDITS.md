# Woodland garden artwork

Generated offline by `scripts/prepare-garden-wide.mjs`. The 1536 × 960 composition
`garden-wide.png` and the derived artwork in this directory are distributed under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), consistent with the
existing garden artwork. Keep the original source credit files with distributions.

## Existing LPC sources

- `oak`, `round`, `willow`, `birch`: cropped and desaturated from the existing
  [LPC Trees Mega-Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-trees).
  Full author list and attribution: `../../source/CREDITS-trees.txt`.
- `bush`, `lavender`, `lily`: cropped and desaturated from the existing
  [LPC Flowers, Plants, Fungi & Wood](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-flowers-plants-fungi-wood).
  Full attribution: `../../source/CREDITS-plants.txt`.
- `bench`: cropped and desaturated from the existing
  [LPC Wooden Furniture](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-wooden-furniture).
  Full attribution: `../../source/CREDITS-furniture.txt`.

## New drawings

`landscape`, `pergola`, `bridge`, `picnic`, `stones`, `fern`, and `reeds` are original
procedural SVG drawings authored for this project with Codex, rasterized with sharp
at half resolution and enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. No external art
was downloaded for these drawings. The script includes their editable source.

## References only

- [Crescent Wing Studio spotlight](https://www.rpgmakerweb.com/blog/resource-spotlight-crescent-wing-studios-mv-tiles):
  architectural focal points and clustered woodland planting. No pixels or tiles reused.
- [Helen Birch's curvy family garden](https://helenbirch.com/projects/curvy-family-garden/):
